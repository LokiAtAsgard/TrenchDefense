Setting & Story

About the Game

"The year is 1914. The Western Front has swallowed three armies and still it hungers."

You are the last field commander of a battered regiment. Your trench is the only thing standing between the enemies' mechanised advance and the supply depot behind the ridge. Every shell crater is a potential firing position. Every minute of quiet is borrowed time.

WW1-Trench Defense is a real-time tower defense game set in the mud and blood of the First World War. You build fortifications, deploy specialist soldiers, manage limited ammunition and medical supplies, and adapt to escalating enemy tactics wave by wave.

Your objective is to win and survive. Hold for eighteen waves. Don't let a single enemy breach the rear line.

What you will gain: Discover deep historical knowledge about the harsh realities and tactical challenges of trench warfare in 1914. Enjoy immersive, high-stakes entertainment that challenges your strategic planning, quick decision-making, and resource coordination under pressure.

How to Play

Game Mechanics

There is no timed planning phase. You can immediately place your defensive towers right after opening the game before starting the incoming enemy wave.

Resource management revolves around Gold. A starting amount of Gold is provided, which you must use wisely to build your initial defense. To earn more Gold and reinforce your line, you must eliminate advancing enemy units.

Base building (defense building) lets you reinforce trench lines, construct barriers, and place sandbag redoubts. Structures degrade as they take damage; repairing them costs a portion of their original Gold value.

Enemies arrive in 18 waves, divided into 6 waves per chapter. Every 6th wave is a crucial boss wave where elite enemy forces commit their full strength to break your lines.

There is no passive experience tracking for individual units. Upgrades are entirely in the hands of the player and can be purchased manually (either in-game during battle or in menu screens) using your Gold resources.

Upgrades directly boost unit capabilities such as fire rate, health, and damage, without restrictive veteran paths.
